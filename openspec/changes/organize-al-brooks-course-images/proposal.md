# Proposal: Organize Al Brooks Course Images into Chapters

## Problem Statement

Currently, the project has a large collection of trading course screenshots stored in `docs/images/`, but these images are not organized in a way that allows learners to easily browse them by topic. The images need to be categorized according to Al Brooks' trading course structure and displayed in the MkDocs project as organized chapters.

## Goals

1. **Organize images** by course chapter and section based on the Al Brooks course structure
2. **Create chapter documentation** with summaries for each course section
3. **Integrate with MkDocs** to display organized content
4. **Provide navigation** that allows learners to browse by topic

## Course Structure

The Al Brooks trading course is organized into 11 major chapters:

| Chapter | Title | Course Numbers |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Getting Started | 01-07 |
| 2 | Charting Analysis | 08-11 |
| 3 | Market Cycle | 12-18 |
| 4 | Support, Resistance, and Basic Patterns | 19-29 |
| 5 | How to Trade Prerequisites | 30-36 |
| 6 | Trading Price Action Patterns | 37-42 |
| 7 | Trading Channels & Ranges | 43-47 |
| 8 | Trading Different Times of the Day | 48 |
| 9 | Swing and Scalp Trading | 49-50 |
| 10 | Managing Losses | 51-52 |

## Success Criteria

- [ ] All images are categorized and accessible by course chapter
- [ ] Each chapter has a summary page with descriptions
- [ ] Navigation is intuitive and follows course progression
- [ ] Existing MkDocs content is preserved and integrated
